Frequently asked questions

Do you only make maps?
No. Maps are part of my portfolio, but I also design estimators, guided journeys, operational tools and product interfaces around data.
How much does it cost?
Every project is unique, so pricing varies. I always provide a detailed, transparent quote before starting. The first conversation is free and non-binding.
How long does it take?
It depends on complexity. Framing or a focused prototype: a few weeks. A fuller tool: 1–2 months. I always give a realistic timeline upfront.
Do users need to be technical?
No. The goal is for non-experts to use the tool on their own. If needed, I support onboarding.
What kinds of data do you work with?
Excel, CSV, databases, open data, APIs… I adapt to your formats. If your data is unusual, we discuss it.
