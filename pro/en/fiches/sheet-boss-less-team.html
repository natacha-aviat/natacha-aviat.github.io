Boss-less team

I designed how a boss-less team works: every classic line-manager duty is replaced by a clear collective mechanism.

Even teammates who were skeptical at first ended up loving it — and clients saw concrete benefits (reliable answers, trust).

❌ Before

  • Bottleneck at the team lead
  • Clients chasing for a reply
  • Power and load concentrated on one person

✅ After

  • Collective triage every Monday → reply that evening
  • Rotating roles, mutual help and pairing
  • Evaluation and raises owned by the collective

From manager… to collective

Each classic team-lead responsibility has a hierarchy-free counterpart.

Classic manager duty Collective replacement
Receive client requests and assign them Contact form + individual inboxes (client’s choice), then a triage meeting every Monday. Clients know they’ll get an answer the following Monday evening — no chasing.
Hierarchy / HR contact Rotating role with a 3- or 6-month mandate.
Day-to-day help, unblocking Peer-to-peer coaching (rotating) every 2 weeks + mutual help in and outside meetings + pairing + regular co-development sessions.
Performance review, growth areas Team NPS (the client evaluates) + annual collective feedback meeting per person.
Allocate the raise budget Structured annual team meeting, consensus (with mature people).
Approve expense reports Circular chain: each person approves someone else’s expenses, who approves the next, and so on.
Communication, showcasing the work Everyone is equally responsible for making the team shine.
Continuous improvement of how we work Quarterly review of rules, working modes and roles.
Organize team days Rotating role, each person’s turn — alternating Paris / Brussels for a bi-located team.
Organizational UX Self-management Team rituals Facilitation
