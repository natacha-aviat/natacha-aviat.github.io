LibéLex

Contracts and letters for liberal nursing practice. The questionnaire covers replacement, collaboration, professional leases, ending a lease, a formal notice to the landlord, and a test for moving to a SELARL. The document is assembled from templates drafted and validated by a partner law firm, then delivered as a PDF.

❌ Before

  • Generic templates, poorly suited to liberal practice
  • Clauses hard to connect to one's own situation
  • Drafting without knowing what the firm actually validated

✅ After

  • Questions that follow the type of contract or letter
  • Clause-by-clause preview, answers still editable
  • PDF delivered at the end of the questionnaire
  • Templates validated by the partner firm

Journey

1. Questionnaire

Questions change with the document. Answers fill the template: length and billing of a replacement, independence in a collaboration, rent and term of a lease.

2. Preview, then PDF

The contract is shown clause by clause. Answers stay editable. The PDF is delivered at the end. The firm validates the templates; it does not review each generated contract.
