Bandcamp Advanced Discovery

Bandcamp discovery tool custom-built for DJ HoldTight. Surgical digging: explore genre crates, filter by cities, switch between new releases, trends and picks — or run a targeted search for albums / artists / labels.

❌ Before

  • Long, scattered digging on Bandcamp
  • Hard to cross genres, cities and freshness
  • Little overview for a busy DJ

✅ After

  • Dedicated Discover / Search interface
  • Genre crates (electro, hip-hop, soul, dub…)
  • City, volume and Digger mode filters
  • Bilingual FR / EN

UX/UI × Data approach

1. Digger framing:

Understand the DJ’s gesture: explore sonic territories quickly without losing track between tags, local scenes and new releases.

2. Digging interface:

Clear journey (Discover vs Search), dense tags, geographic filters and actionable results — designed for frequent, focused use.
