ANY agenda

An interface for Accueil New York's public calendar. Meetings read week by week, from today through 31 August.

❌ Before

  • The official calendar is a long list
  • Finding one activity means scrolling through everything
  • Waitlist and full status sit inside each event page

✅ After

  • One row per week, with each day's date
  • Saved filters: category, day, time, place, neighborhood
  • Waitlist and full status visible on the card
