DecimalClock

A web clock that shows classic time and its decimal equivalent side by side. One day = 10 units: midnight is 0•00, noon is 5•00.

❌ Before

  • Decimal time stays abstract (French Revolution curiosity)
  • No visual anchor against 24 h / 60 min
  • Manual conversions feel opaque

✅ After

  • Two dials synced in real time
  • Duration scales shown (unit ↔ hours / minutes)
  • Instant reading: 12 h = 5 units

How it works

Classic side

24 hours, with AM / PM rings and familiar readout.

Decimal side

10 units per day; 1 unit = 2 h 24; 1 tenth = 14 min 24 s.
