MaMaCollecte

Web tool for association MaMaMa: let volunteers sign up for supermarket collection slots (baby food and hygiene products), with location filters and remaining capacity.

❌ Before

  • Sign-ups handled manually (messages, spreadsheets)
  • Slots overbooked or empty by location
  • Little visibility on one’s own bookings

✅ After

  • Pick a location + browse available slots
  • Remaining capacity shown live
  • Confirm and track sign-ups by phone number

Volunteer journey

1. Locations

Select one or more collection points.

2. Slots

Browse the calendar and see remaining capacity.

3. Sign-up

Enter contact details and confirm the slot.
